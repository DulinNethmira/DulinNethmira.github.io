## 💡 Some tips

- **Start on the lower end** to build your portfolio. Your first 3-5 clients are basically portfolio pieces.
- **Offer package deals** like "website + 5 social media videos" for 25,000-40,000. Bundles feel like a better deal to small business owners.

- **Charge per project, not per hour.** Local businesses don't think in hourly rates.
- **Get 50% upfront** before starting any work. This is normal and protects you.
